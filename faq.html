Pricing and contracts

How much does a project cost? +

It depends on size, site and finishes. After a short survey we give a fixed price, listed line by line, so you can compare it against any other bid.

Do you offer fixed-price contracts? +

Yes. Our standard contract is fixed-price. Prices change only if you ask for a change in scope, and we agree that change in writing first.

How do payments work? +

Payments follow inspected progress, not the calendar. Each milestone is photographed and signed off before we invoice.

Timelines and permits

How long will my project take? +

A single home usually takes 8 to 12 months. A mid-size commercial building takes 14 to 24 months. We give a dated programme with every bid.

Do you handle permits and approvals? +

Yes. Our team prepares and submits planning, environmental and utility applications, and tracks them for you.

What happens if the monsoon delays work? +

Our programmes include weather allowances based on ten years of site records. If delays go beyond them, we tell you straight away and explain the recovery plan.

Quality and warranty

What warranty do you provide? +

Ten years on structure and waterproofing, and one year on finishes and building services. We respond to warranty calls within 48 hours.

Who supervises the work on site? +

Every project has a named site manager and a licensed engineer. You get a written progress report with photos every Friday.

Can I visit the site during construction? +

Yes. Book a visit with your site manager, who will give you the safety briefing and protective gear.
